## Anti-Patterns Verdict

LLM: loudest tells removed (Tailwind palette, rainbow badges, card grids, flat hierarchy). Residual: canonical chat-onboarding structure; uniform roadmap card stack. Detector (4 findings, frontend/src/index.css): overused-font (Space Grotesk + Inter, committed in DESIGN.md); 3x transition:width (lines 325, 771, 1323 — progress fills, use transform: scaleX). No false positives. No browser overlay (no automation available).

## Priority Issues

- [P1] Delete control keyboard-inaccessible: span role=button with onClick only; Enter/Space inert. Fix: onKeyDown handler. → harden
- [P1] Completing the roadmap ends flat: no completion state; peak-end violated for a momentum product. Fix: fully-lit rail moment + header celebration. → delight
- [P1] Onboarding rigid/slow: 1.5s typing delay x9, no skip; demo wow ~90s deep; returning users pay every time. Fix: shorten after first exchange, skip on re-answer. → onboard
- [P2] Upcoming phase cards full density: confidence/notes/buttons on every future card; wall of sameness. Fix: collapse upcoming to header, expand when current. → distill
- [P2] Consistency/contrast debts: Continue button should be brass; placeholders #49585d ~2.7:1 too low; transition:width x3. → polish

## Persona Red Flags

- Sam: Delete focusable but inert on Enter/Space; placeholder guidance at ~2.7:1; progressbar ARIA present (good).
- The Judge (project persona): signature visual gated behind full Q&A; no sample roadmap for instant demo.
